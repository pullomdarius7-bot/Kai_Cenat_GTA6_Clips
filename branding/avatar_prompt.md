**How to use:** open ChatGPT (image generation). Upload `avatar_current.png` (your current picture) as the reference image, then paste the prompt below.

---

## Prompt (copy everything in the box)

```
Use the uploaded image as the character reference. Keep the SAME character: same face shape, skin tone, beard shape, long black twisted locs, small diamond stud earrings, layered silver chains, grey zip-up tracksuit jacket, and the gold crown floating above his head. Same bold cartoon illustration style with clean black outlines.

Changes:
- Reframe to a head-and-chest close-up. His face should fill about 45% of the image, centered, with the top of the crown just inside the frame.
- Give him a big, genuine laugh: mouth wide open, showing teeth, eyes squinted with joy, head tilted slightly back, one hand coming up toward his chest like he just saw something hilarious. He looks like he's having the time of his life.
- Remove the room, couch, books, mug, signs, computer, and all text.
- Background: solid near-black, with a thick circular paint-splash ring behind his head and shoulders in red, yellow, and green (matching the original ring), with a few paint splatters.
- Soft warm light on his face and a thin yellow rim light on his locs so he separates from the dark background.

Square 1:1 image, 1080x1080. Everything important must stay inside the center circle because it will be cropped round on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.

Avoid: any text or words, logos, extra fingers, a changed face or different person, neon light streaks, glowing particles, lens flares, plastic-looking skin, busy background details.
```

## If you'd rather have "surprised" instead of laughing
Swap the laugh bullet for this one:
```
- Give him a big surprised, hyped expression: eyebrows raised high, eyes wide, mouth open in an "O" like "NO WAY", both hands up near his face. Fun and excited, not scared.
```

## Checking the result before you use it
1. Shrink it on your phone to thumbnail size. You should still be able to tell he's laughing.
2. Make sure the crown and the top of his locs aren't cut off when it's shown as a circle.
3. Check his hands. AI tools often add extra fingers. If they look wrong, ask ChatGPT: "same image, fix the hands to have five fingers".
4. Use the same picture on all three platforms so people recognize you everywhere.
